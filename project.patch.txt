+# Zerodha Kite: Helping First-Time Investors Avoid Early Losses Without Slowing Down Pros
+
+**A product management case study on designing zero-latency "Training Wheels" for beginner investors on Zerodha Kite.**
+
+> *Independent case study. Not affiliated with Zerodha. Feature ideas are illustrative and are not investment advice.*
+
+---
+
+## ⚡ TL;DR (2-Minute Recruiter Summary)
+* **Problem:** First-time investors lose money and trust early because Kite’s Order Pad uses pro-trader abbreviations (`MIS`, `CNC`, `SL-M`) and exposes 5x intraday leverage by default—leading to accidental intraday trades, ₹50 + GST auto-square-off penalties at 3:20 PM `[SOURCE]`, and fee-shock on small orders.
+* **Target User:** First-time Indian retail investors aged 18–25 starting with ₹5,000–₹10,000 per month *(Assumption)*.
+* **Solution ("Kite Training Wheels"):** An adaptive, client-side UI layer active *only* for a user's first 25 executed trades. It adds plain-English order subtitles, inline 15-second Varsity cards, and a pre-trade "Sanity Check" bottom sheet using Zerodha's existing Nudge framework.
+* **Key Metric:** **90-Day Healthy Active Beginner Rate** (Target: `+5% to +8%` relative lift *(Assumption)* with **0ms degradation** to p99 order execution latency).
+* **My Role:** Solo PM & Technical Architect (Final-year CS student). Conducted user discovery, mapped the journey, prioritized via RICE, wrote the PRD, designed client-side zero-latency architecture, and structured the A/B test.

+## 1. Problem Statement & Why It Matters
+Zerodha is built for speed, reliability, and zero spam `[SOURCE]`. It charges ₹0 brokerage on Equity Delivery (`CNC`) and makes the vast majority of its revenue from flat ₹20 fees on Intraday (`MIS`) and F&O orders `[SOURCE]`.
+
+However, when young beginners join Zerodha for its brand trust, they land in a cockpit built for experienced traders.
+
+* **Why it matters for Users:** If a 22-year-old starting with ₹5,000 accidentally selects `MIS` (Intraday) instead of `CNC` (Long-term Delivery) because `MIS` asks for 5x less upfront margin, their stock gets forcefully sold at 3:20 PM with a ₹50 + 18% GST penalty `[SOURCE]`. They feel cheated and quit investing early.
+* **Why it matters for Zerodha:**
+  * **10-Year LTV:** A 22-year-old with ₹5,000 today becomes a 32-year-old investor with ₹20 Lakhs *(Assumption)*. Early churn destroys long-term Account Maintenance Charges (AMC), Coin mutual fund adoption, and trading LTV.
+  * **Market Share Defense:** Competitors like Groww have overtaken Zerodha in active client count largely by capturing first-time investors with a simpler interface `[SOURCE]`.
+  * **SEBI Regulatory Alignment:** SEBI studies show over 90% of individual F&O/speculative retail traders lose money `[SOURCE]`. Protecting beginners aligns with regulatory expectations.
+

+## 2. User Research & Key Insights
+* **Method:** 10-question behavioral interview guide ([view guide](./research/interview-guide.md)) targeting 8–10 beginner investors aged 18–25, supplemented by secondary analysis of Kite Google Play Store reviews, `r/IndiaInvestments`, and `TradingQnA` threads `[SOURCE]`.
+* **Participants (Anonymized Sample):**
+  * `Participant 1 (Age 21, Bengaluru - CS Student)`: Used Kite & Groww. Did not understand `CNC` vs `MIS`. Bought Intraday by mistake and got hit with auto-square-off charges. Quote: *"I thought MIS was cheaper because it asked for less money."*
+  * *(See [./research/findings.md](./research/findings.md) for full participant log).*
+
+**Top 3 Research Insights:**
+1. **The "Cheap Stock" Margin Illusion:** Beginners see 5x lower "Margin Required" on `MIS` and assume it is a discount, unknowingly taking 5x leveraged intraday risk.
+2. **Death by Small-Ticket Charges:** Beginners buying and selling ₹300–₹500 worth of shares in Delivery (`CNC`) don't realize that Depository Participant (DP) charges (₹13.5 + 18% GST on sell) `[SOURCE]` turn a ₹10 stock gain into a net loss.
+3. **The Varsity Silo:** Zerodha owns India's best free financial education platform (*Varsity*), but zero Varsity context appears inside Kite where order decisions happen.

+## 5. Solution Design: "Kite Training Wheels"
+* **Option A: Dual-Mode App ("Investor Mode" vs. "Trader Mode")** — *Rejected.* Violates Zerodha’s lean engineering culture by forcing maintenance of two UIs and hides the learning path.
+* **Option B: "Kite Training Wheels" (Adaptive Labels + Pre-Trade Sanity Check + Varsity Bite)** — **SELECTED.** Active only for accounts with `< 25 lifetime executed orders`. Auto-graduates at trade #26 and has a 1-tap permanent opt-out for pros.
+* **Option C: AI Copilot Stock Advisor** — *Rejected.* Severe SEBI compliance risk; recommending whether to buy/sell a stock crosses into unlicensed Investment Advisory (RIA/RA) `[SOURCE]`.
+

+## 6. PRD Summary & Technical Architecture
+📄 **[Read the Full One-Page PRD in `./analysis/prd.md`](./analysis/prd.md)**
+
+* **Eligibility:** Default ON for users with `< 25 lifetime equity trades`. Can be toggled OFF in 1 tap on the Order Pad or in Settings.
+* **Core Features:**
+  1. **Dynamic Plain-English Subtitles:** Explains `Intraday (MIS)` (5x margin, 3:20 PM cutoff, ₹50+GST penalty) vs. `Longterm (CNC)` right under the toggle.
+  2. **Pre-Trade "Sanity Check" Bottom Sheet:** Triggers *only* on a beginner's first 3 `MIS` orders OR when round-trip charges (including ₹15.93 DP charges) exceed `3%` of a small order value. Offers a 1-tap button to switch to `Longterm (CNC)`.
+  3. **Inline "Varsity Bite" Drawer:** Tapping `(?)` opens a native 15-second card explaining `Market vs. Limit` orders without losing order state.
+* **💻 Zero-Latency Architecture (CS Implementation):**
+  * At login, the session payload caches `is_beginner_mode: true` (`lifetime_trades < 25`) in local device storage (`SharedPreferences`/`SQLite`).
+  * Fee and break-even calculations are deterministic formulas executed **100% client-side in `< 0.2ms`** with **zero extra network API calls** during order placement.
+* **SEBI Compliance:** Explains **100% order mechanics and statutory charges**, **0% stock quality or return predictions**.

+## 9. Experiment Design (A/B Test)
+📄 **[Full A/B Test Math & Volatility Controls in `./analysis/ab-test-design.md`](./analysis/ab-test-design.md)**
+
+* **Setup:** Concurrent 50/50 user-level A/B test on newly activated accounts placing their first mobile equity trade.
+* **Primary Decision Proxy:** **30-Day Healthy Repeat Trade Rate** (`>= 3 trades in 30 days with 0 auto-square-off penalties`).
+* **Sample Size:** Assuming a `40%` baseline *(Assumption)*, `5%` relative MDE (`40% -> 42%`), `95%` confidence, and `80%` power requires **~9,500 users per arm (~19,000 total)** over a **6-week window** (2 weeks recruitment + 30 days observation).
+* **Handling Market Volatility:** Running Control and Variant **strictly concurrently** with stratified sampling (separating IPO sign-ups from organic equity sign-ups) ensures market dips hit both buckets equally.

+## 10. Risks, Trade-Offs & What I Chose Not to Build
+* **Revenue Trade-Off (Short-Term ₹20 vs. Long-Term LTV):** Steering beginners out of accidental Intraday (`₹20` fee) into Delivery (`₹0` fee) dips Day-1 brokerage slightly. We accept this trade-off because preventing early blow-ups preserves multi-year AMC, Coin, and active trader LTV.
+* **Paternalism vs. Freedom:** Mitigated by auto-graduating after 25 trades, capping the bottom sheet at 3 `MIS` orders, and offering a 1-tap permanent opt-out.
+* **What I Deliberately Did NOT Build:**
+  1. ❌ *No Gamified Streaks or Badges* (encourages over-trading; dark pattern).
+  2. ❌ *No Social/Community Copy-Trading Feed* (fuels FOMO and pump-and-dump risk).
+  3. ❌ *No AI "Safe Stocks for Beginners" List* (violates SEBI advisory regulations).
+
+---
+
+## 11. What I'd Do Differently & Learnings
+* **Limitation / Next Steps:** "Kite Training Wheels" prevents *operational execution mistakes* on the Order Pad, but beginners can still lose money by buying hyped penny stocks in Delivery (`CNC`). In V2, I would surface Zerodha's existing fundamental risk flags (high promoter pledging, ASM/GSM surveillance warnings) more clearly for beginners before they open the Order Pad.
+* **Key Learning:** Building for fintech requires balancing three forces simultaneously—**user protection**, **unit economics**, and **SEBI regulatory boundaries**—while respecting engineering constraints like client-side latency.

+# Opportunity Prioritization (RICE Framework)
+
+> *Independent case study. Not affiliated with Zerodha. Feature ideas are illustrative and are not investment advice.*
+
+## Formula
+$$\text{RICE Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$$
+
+* **Cohort Baseline:** 100,000 new beginner accounts per month *(Assumption)*.
+* **Impact Scale:** `0.25` (Minimal), `0.5` (Low), `1.0` (Medium), `2.0` (High), `3.0` (Massive).
+* **Effort:** Engineering and design person-months.
+
+## RICE Scoring Table
+
+| Opportunity ("How Might We...") | Reach *(Assumption)* | Impact | Confidence | Effort (Person-Mos) | Working & Final RICE Score |
+| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
+| **HMW #1: Plain-English Order Pad Labels** *(Auto-off after 25 trades)* | **95,000** *(95% of active beginners open Order Pad)* | **2.0** *(Directly stops MIS vs CNC confusion)* | **90%** | **1.5** *(Simple frontend label & toggle)* | `(95,000 × 2 × 0.9) / 1.5` = **114,000 (Rank 1)** |
+| **HMW #2: Pre-Trade "Sanity Check" Nudge** *(Leverage & Fee Alert)* | **80,000** *(80% attempt small-ticket or intraday orders)* | **3.0** *(Prevents direct loss & ₹50 penalty)* | **85%** | **2.5** *(Extends existing Nudge engine)* | `(80,000 × 3 × 0.85) / 2.5` = **81,600 (Rank 2)** |
+| **HMW #3: Contextual Varsity Micro-Cards** | **60,000** *(60% tap info icons)* | **1.0** *(Improves knowledge; indirect impact)* | **80%** | **2.0** *(Content mapping + bottom drawer)* | `(60,000 × 1 × 0.8) / 2.0` = **24,000 (Rank 3)** |
+| **HMW #5: Post-Trade Loss / Fee Breakdown** | **50,000** *(50% lose money or pay high % fees on Trade 1)* | **1.0** *(Reactive—after money is already lost)* | **70%** | **3.0** *(Requires Console back-office sync)* | `(50,000 × 1 × 0.7) / 3.0` = **11,667 (Rank 4)** |
+| **HMW #4: Full "Kite Lite" Beginner Mode** | **70,000** *(70% opt-in during onboarding)* | **2.0** *(Clean UI, but hides growth path)* | **60%** | **8.0** *(Heavy effort maintaining two UIs)* | `(70,000 × 2 × 0.6) / 8.0` = **10,500 (Rank 5)** |
+| **HMW #6: Paper Trading Sandbox inside Kite** | **40,000** *(40% try virtual trading)* | **1.0** *(Users take fake risks with fake money)* | **50%** | **10.0** *(Backend load + SEBI rules)* | `(40,000 × 1 × 0.5) / 10.0` = **2,000 (Rank 6)** |
+
+## Justification for Final Selection
+We combine **HMW #1** and **HMW #2** (supported by lightweight links from **HMW #3**) into **"Kite Training Wheels"**:
+1. **Prevention Beats Cure:** Intercepting an accidental 5x leveraged `MIS` trade *before* execution saves user capital immediately.
+2. **Respects Zerodha's Lean Tech Philosophy:** Avoids maintaining a bloated "Kite Lite" fork and respects SEBI restrictions on gamified virtual/paper trading `[SOURCE]`.

+# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Kite Training Wheels
+
+> *Independent case study. Not affiliated with Zerodha. Feature ideas are illustrative and are not investment advice.*
+
+* **Feature Name:** Kite Training Wheels
+* **Platform:** Kite Mobile App (Android & iOS)
+* **Status:** Draft Proposal | **Target Segment:** First-time investors (`< 25 lifetime executed trades`)
+
+---
+
+## 1. Problem & Goal
+* **Problem:** Beginners (`< 25 trades`) lose money and trust early because pro-trader abbreviations (`MIS`, `CNC`, `SL-M`) and hidden fee mechanics on small orders cause accidental leveraged trades, ₹50 + GST auto-square-off penalties at 3:20 PM `[SOURCE]`, and net-negative returns due to ₹13.5 + GST DP charges `[SOURCE]`.
+* **Goal:** Reduce accidental intraday orders, auto-square-off penalties, and 90-day beginner churn while adding **0ms of latency** for experienced users.
+
+## 2. Eligibility & Graduation Logic
+* Enabled by default when `user.lifetime_executed_equity_orders < 25`.
+* Automatically turns off at trade #26 with a dismissible toast notification.
+* Can be permanently turned OFF in 1 tap via the Order Pad header (`"Turn off hints"`) or toggled anytime in `Settings -> Order Preferences`.
+
+## 3. User Stories
+1. **Plain-English Subtitles:** *As a beginner on the Order Pad*, I want to see a 1-line explanation under `Intraday (MIS)` and `Longterm (CNC)` so I know which option holds the stock overnight and which option auto-sells at 3:20 PM.
+2. **Upfront Small-Order Fee Guard:** *As a small-budget beginner placing an order under ₹500*, I want to see if round-trip statutory and DP charges exceed `3%` of my order value *before* I swipe to buy.
+3. **Pre-Trade Sanity Check:** *As a beginner selecting Intraday (`MIS`) for the first 3 times*, I want a quick confirmation card explaining the 5x leverage exposure and ₹50 + GST auto-square-off rule, with a 1-tap button to switch to `Longterm (CNC)`.
+4. **Inline Varsity Bite:** *As a curious beginner*, I want to tap `(?)` next to order types (`Market`, `Limit`, `SL`) to read a 15-second native summary without leaving my order ticket.
+
+## 4. Scope
+* **IN SCOPE:** Mobile Order Pad adaptive subtitles, pre-trade Sanity Check bottom sheet (capped at first 3 `MIS` orders or when fees `> 3%` of trade value), native Varsity Bite bottom drawer, 1-tap permanent opt-out.
+* **OUT OF SCOPE:** Stock quality ratings or buy/sell recommendations (prohibited by SEBI `[SOURCE]`), Kite Web changes (Phase 1 is Mobile only), any UI changes for accounts with `>= 25` trades.
+
+## 5. Edge Cases
+* **Pro User on a New Account:** If an experienced trader opens a family account, they can tap `"Turn off hints"` on their very first order screen in 1 tap.
+* **Small Screens (5-inch phones):** The 2-line helper banner collapses into 1 line when the numeric keyboard opens so the `"Swipe to Buy"` button is never pushed off-screen.
+* **Fast-Moving Markets:** The Sanity Check bottom sheet includes a `"Don't show this check again"` checkbox and automatically stops after 3 `MIS` trades.
+
+## 6. Technical Architecture (Zero-Latency Design)
+* **Local Session Flag:** On login, the user profile payload includes `is_beginner_mode: boolean` (evaluated asynchronously on backend via `lifetime_trades < 25`) and caches it in local device storage (`SharedPreferences` / `SQLite`).
+* **Client-Side Fee Math:** Brokerage, STT, exchange fees, and DP charge estimates are deterministic mathematical formulas computed **100% on the phone CPU in `< 0.2ms`**.
+* **Zero Extra Network Calls:** Varsity micro-card text and fee parameters are synced via a lightweight remote config JSON on app launch, ensuring **0 network API calls** when opening or swiping the Order Pad.
+
+## 7. SEBI Regulatory Compliance
+* Under SEBI Investment Adviser (IA) and Research Analyst (RA) regulations `[SOURCE]`, brokers cannot offer unapproved buy/sell advice on specific securities.
+* All copy in "Kite Training Wheels" strictly describes **exchange rules, order mechanics, and statutory charges**—never the underlying stock's prospects.

+# Experiment Design (A/B Test Specification)
+
+> *Independent case study. Not affiliated with Zerodha. Feature ideas are illustrative and are not investment advice.*
+
+## 1. Hypothesis
+If we show adaptive plain-English order labels and a pre-trade Sanity Check bottom sheet (**"Kite Training Wheels"**) to new users during their first 25 trades, then their **30-day healthy repeat trade rate** and **90-day retention** will increase because fewer beginners will suffer accidental intraday penalties or fee-shock losses.
+
+## 2. Experiment Setup
+* **Randomization Unit:** User-level (`user_id`) for newly activated Zerodha accounts placing their first mobile equity trade.
+* **Control (50%):** Standard Kite Mobile Order Pad.
+* **Variant (50%):** Kite Order Pad with "Kite Training Wheels" enabled for `< 25` lifetime trades.
+
+## 3. Metrics
+* **Primary Decision Metric (Fast-Feedback Proxy):** **30-Day Healthy Repeat Trade Rate** (`% of users completing >= 3 trades in their first 30 days with 0 auto-square-off penalties`).
+* **Secondary Metric:** First-30-day 3:20 PM Auto-Square-Off incidence.
+* **Guardrails:**
+  1. *Order Pad Completion Rate* (`Orders Placed / Order Pad Opened`) must not drop by `> 2%`.
+  2. *p99 Order Execution Latency* must show `0ms` statistically significant regression.
+
+## 4. Sample Size & Duration Logic
+* **Inputs:**
+  * Control Baseline Rate: `40.0%` *(Assumption)*
+  * Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE): `5% relative lift` (`40.0% -> 42.0%`)
+  * Statistical Significance ($\alpha$): `95% confidence`
+  * Statistical Power ($1 - \beta$): `80% power`
+* **Required Sample Size:** **~9,500 users per arm (~19,000 users total)** *(Assumption based on standard two-proportion z-test)*.
+* **Duration:** Assuming ~3,000 eligible first-time equity traders per day *(Assumption)*, we recruit users across **14 days** (to capture two full weekly market cycles) + **30 days** observation window = **6 weeks total duration**.
+
+## 5. Ship / Iterate / Kill Decision Rule
+* 🟢 **SHIP:** Primary Metric lifts by `>= +5% relative`, auto-square-off penalties drop by `>= 25%`, and Guardrails remain within bounds.
+* 🟡 **ITERATE:** Auto-square-off penalties drop by `>= 30%`, *but* Order Pad Completion Rate drops by `> 5%` (indicating our bottom-sheet warning copy is too alarming). *Action:* Soften copy and test an inline banner vs. modal sheet.
+* 🔴 **KILL:** Primary Metric shows `0%` or negative lift, and `> 50%` of users dismiss or disable the hints immediately.
+
+## 6. Controlling for Market Volatility & Seasonality
+Stock trading volume fluctuates heavily during macro events (Union Budget, RBI rate decisions, earnings season, market crashes).
+1. **Strict Concurrent Testing:** Never use before-vs-after comparisons; run Control and Variant simultaneously so market volatility affects both groups identically.
+2. **Stratified Sampling:** Balance IPO-acquired users and organic equity sign-ups equally across Control and Variant.
+3. **Anomaly Blackouts:** Avoid starting experiment recruitment on outlier trading sessions (e.g., Budget Day or Diwali Muhurat Trading).
