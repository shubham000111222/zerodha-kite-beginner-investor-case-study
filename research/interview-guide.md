# User Research Interview Guide

> *Independent case study. Not affiliated with Zerodha. Feature ideas are illustrative and are not investment advice.*

## Objective
Understand how first-time investors (ages 18–25) navigate their first few trades on the Zerodha Kite mobile app, what terminology confuses them, and how early operational mistakes affect their trust.

## Target Participants
* **Segment:** 8–10 beginner retail investors aged 18–25.
* **Criteria:** Opened a Demat account within the last 6–12 months; starting capital between ₹5,000 and ₹10,000 per month *(Assumption)*.
* **Rule:** Avoid leading questions (e.g., *"Do you want a beginner mode?"*). Focus strictly on past behavior.

## 10 Interview Questions
1. Walk me through why you decided to open a Zerodha account instead of Groww or Angel One.
2. Tell me about the very first stock you bought on Kite. What was going through your mind on the Buy screen?
3. Have you ever placed an order on Kite that behaved differently than you expected? What happened?
4. Look at this screenshot of the Kite Buy screen *(show order window)*. In your own words, what do `MIS`, `CNC`, `SL`, and `GTT` mean to you?
5. When you don't understand a term inside Kite, what do you currently do?
6. Have you ever tried Intraday trading or F&O? What made you try it, and what was the outcome?
7. Have you ever been surprised by the charges or taxes on your Console report after making a trade?
8. Have you heard of Zerodha Varsity? If yes, how often do you use it while trading?
9. What is the single most stressful moment for you when using the Kite app?
10. Have you ever stopped using Kite for a few weeks or switched to another app? Why?
